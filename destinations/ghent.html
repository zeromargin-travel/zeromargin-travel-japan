Top 10 Highlights in This Route

Below are the handpicked venues included in this walking tour. Every location has been verified for quality and geographic proximity.

★ ★4.8
Landmark

1. Gravensteen

Formidable 12th-century moated stone castle built by Count Philip of Alsace featuring battlements, deep moat, and medieval torture instruments.

💡 Insider Tip: 🎧 The audio guide is widely praised for its humor; climb to the top of the keep for a sweeping skyline view.
★ ★4.8
Landmark

2. Sint-Baafskathedraal

Monumental Gothic cathedral housing the world-famous 15th-century masterpiece The Adoration of the Mystic Lamb (Ghent Altarpiece) by Van Eyck.

💡 Insider Tip: 👓 The dedicated visitor center uses AR headsets in the Romanesque crypt to reconstruct the turbulent history of the altarpiece.
★ ★4.9
Museum & Gallery

3. Het Lam Gods

Painted in 1432, this 12-panel polyptych altarpiece is celebrated as one of the most influential masterworks in global art history.

💡 Insider Tip: 🔍 Use high-resolution interactive touchscreens inside the gallery to zoom in on micro-details like pearls and reflections.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

4. Het Belfort van Gent

91-meter Gothic tower (UNESCO) symbolizing civic independence, crowned by a legendary gilded copper dragon weather vane and 54-bell carillon.

💡 Insider Tip: 🪜 A modern elevator runs from the 1st floor directly up to the panoramic observation gallery for easy access.
★ ★4.7
Scenery & Walk

5. Korenmarkt

Ghent's central public square, historically the center of grain trade, framed by Saint Nicholas Church and former Main Post Office.

💡 Insider Tip: ☕ Walk inside De Post to view preserved 19th-century clock tower architecture and grab coffee at Pain Quotidien.
★ ★4.8
Scenery & Walk

6. Patershol

Labyrinthine medieval quarter located just north of Castle of the Counts, Ghent's premier culinary neighborhood of cobbled lanes.

💡 Insider Tip: 🍽️ The narrow streets of Oudburg are packed with independent eateries; make reservations for Gentse Waterzooi.
★ ★4.9
Scenery & Walk

7. Sint-Michielsbrug

Arched stone bridge crossing Leie river, celebrated as the single best panoramic viewpoint in Ghent framing the 'Three Towers'.

💡 Insider Tip: 📸 Stand at the highest point of the bridge facing east for the iconic 3-tower alignment photo, then pivot 180 degrees.
★ ★4.6
Landmark

8. Sint-Niklaaskerk

13th-century masterpiece of Tournai Gothic architecture built in local blue-grey limestone, situated on Korenmarkt square.

💡 Insider Tip: ⛪ Free admission; admire the historic Aristide Cavaillé-Coll romantic pipe organ and explore quiet side chapels.
★ ★4.7
Scenery & Walk

9. Boottochten Gent

Open-top passenger boat tours navigating Leie and Lieve canals departing from Graslei and Korenlei docks with medieval views.

💡 Insider Tip: 🚤 The 40-minute guided tours run year-round; departures from Korenlei docks generally offer multilingual live commentary.
★ ★4.6
Scenery & Walk

10. Werregarenstraat

Narrow pedestrian alley serving as an officially sanctioned public canvas for street artists, where spray-painted murals change daily.

💡 Insider Tip: 📸 Vibrant photo backdrop that is never the same twice; walk through during daylight hours for natural lighting.

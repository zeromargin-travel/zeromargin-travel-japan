Top 10 Highlights in This Route

Below are the handpicked venues included in this walking tour. Every location has been verified for quality and geographic proximity.

★ ★4.8
Landmark

1. Place de la Bourse & Miroir d'eau

18th-century grand square set along the riverfront, home to the world's largest water mirror reflecting classical facades.

💡 Insider Tip: Visit at dusk when the 18th-century palace facade illuminates and reflects perfectly in the 2cm shallow water mirror.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

2. Grand Théâtre de Bordeaux

Masterpiece of 18th-century Neoclassical architecture featuring a portico of 12 Corinthian columns topped by muses and goddesses.

💡 Insider Tip: Famed for its 12 Corinthian columns topped with statues of Muses and Goddesses. Inspired the design of the Paris Opera Garnier!
★ ★4.7
Landmark

3. Cathédrale Saint-André & Tour Pey-Berland

Gothic cathedral where Eleanor of Aquitaine married Louis VII, paired with a freestanding bell tower offering city views.

💡 Insider Tip: Climb the 229 steps of the standalone Pey-Berland bell tower for THE ultimate 360-degree view over Bordeaux.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

4. Porte Cailhau

15th-century white stone city gate standing 35 meters tall, built to commemorate King Charles VIII's victory at Fornovo.

💡 Insider Tip: 15th-century fairytale medieval gate. Climb inside to see historical defense displays and views over the Garonne river.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

5. Grosse Cloche

Historic 15th-century belfry of the former town hall, featuring twin conical towers and a massive 7,800kg bronze bell.

💡 Insider Tip: One of France's oldest belfries featuring a 7.8-ton giant bronze bell 'Armande-Louise' cast in 1775.
★ ★4.6
Café & Bistro

6. Marché des Capucins

Bustling indoor market known as 'the belly of Bordeaux', famous for fresh Arcachon oysters paired with chilled white wine.

💡 Insider Tip: Bordeaux's bustling central food market. Enjoy fresh oysters from Arcachon paired with a glass of dry white wine at Sunday lunch!
★ ★4.7
Scenery & Walk

7. Quartier Saint-Pierre

Historic heart of old Bordeaux with narrow cobblestone alleys, shaded squares, wine bars, and lively restaurant terraces.

💡 Insider Tip: Charming medieval pedestrian cobblestone district packed with wine bars, bistros, and small squares near Place du Parlement.
★ ★4.6
Landmark

8. Basilique Saint-Michel & Flèche Saint-Michel

Flamboyant Gothic basilica crowned by a freestanding 114m spire, one of the tallest in southern France.

💡 Insider Tip: The standalone Gothic spire 'Flèche Saint-Michel' (114m) is the 2nd tallest bell tower in France. Explore the vintage flea market on Place Saint-Michel below.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

9. Monument aux Girondins

Monumental 54-meter column topped by a bronze statue of Liberty, flanked by dramatic fountains honoring the Girondin martyrs.

💡 Insider Tip: Europe's largest square featuring a 54m column with sea horses and bronze fountain statues symbolizing Liberty.
★ ★4.5
Landmark

10. Palais Gallien

Atmospheric arches and wall ruins of a 2nd-century Roman amphitheatre that once seated 15,000 spectators.

💡 Insider Tip: Remains of a 2nd-century Roman amphitheatre tucked in a residential neighbourhood—free to view from the street edge.

Top 10 Highlights in This Route

Below are the handpicked venues included in this walking tour. Every location has been verified for quality and geographic proximity.

★ ★4.7
Landmark

1. Tour Eiffel

Iconic 330m iron lattice tower offering panoramic views of Paris.

💡 Insider Tip: 🎟️ Book summit tickets online 60 days in advance! If sold out, walk up the stairs to 2nd floor for shorter lines, then buy the summit elevator ticket there.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

2. Arc de Triomphe

Triumphal arch honoring those who fought for France, set atop Champs-Élysées.

💡 Insider Tip: 🎟️ Access via the underground pedestrian tunnel (never attempt to cross the chaotic roundabout above ground)! Climb to the rooftop 30 minutes before sunset for breathtaking 360° views down the 12 radiating avenues and the sparkling Eiffel Tower.
★ ★4.8
Landmark

3. Sainte-Chapelle

13th-century Gothic royal chapel famed for 1,113 soaring stained glass windows.

💡 Insider Tip: 🎟️ Visit between 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM on a sunny day when sunlight floods the 1,113 13th-century stained glass windows. Mandatory timed online security ticket.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

4. Cathédrale Notre-Dame de Paris

Masterpiece of French Gothic architecture on Île de la Cité.

💡 Insider Tip: 🎟️ Fully reopened in December 2024! Reserve free timed entry tickets via the official app. Best facade photography is from Pont Saint-Louis or Pont de l'Archevêché.
★ ★4.5
Museum & Art

5. Musée Picasso Paris

Nestled in the historic Le Marais district, the Musée Picasso Paris is housed within the grand Hôtel Salé, a beautifully restored 17th-century aristocratic mansion. The museum boasts an extraordinary collection of over 5,000 of Pablo Picasso's works spanning his early to late periods, masterfully displayed alongside the artist’s own remarkable private collection of pieces by masters like Cézanne and Matisse.

💡 Insider Tip: 💡 Take your time exploring this stunning 17th-century mansion; beyond Picasso's personal masterpieces spanning his entire career, do not miss his fascinating private collection featuring works by revered artists such as Cézanne and Matisse.
★ ★4.4
Café & Bakery

6. Les Deux Magots

A legendary Saint-Germain café that served as the intellectual hub for literary giants like Ernest Hemingway, Simone de Beauvoir, and Jean-Paul Sartre.

💡 Insider Tip: Skip the standard coffee and order their legendary, impossibly thick hot chocolate, elegantly served in porcelain pots on the historic outdoor terrace.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

7. Sacré-Cœur Basilica

Immerse yourself in the captivating charm of Sacré-Cœur Basilica located in the heart of Paris, France. This iconic destination blends history, culture, and vibrant energy to create an unforgettable experience.

💡 Insider Tip: To fully appreciate Sacré-Cœur Basilica, plan your visit during the early morning or late afternoon to avoid peak crowds and witness the stunning lighting. Don't forget your camera for breathtaking shots!
★ ★4.6
Landmark

8. Palais-Royal Courtyard & Gardens

17th-century palace courtyard featuring Buren's striped columns and rose gardens.

💡 Insider Tip: 📸 Walk across Pont de l'Archevêché or Square Jean-XXIII behind the cathedral for the best restored Gothic facade view along the Seine.
★ ★4.6
Landmark

9. Panthéon Paris

Neoclassical mausoleum containing tombs of Victor Hugo, Voltaire & Marie Curie.

💡 Insider Tip: ☀️ Visit on a sunny day between 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM when sunlight directly penetrates the 15-meter 13th-century stained glass windows upstairs.
★ ★4.8
Landmark

10. Jardin du Luxembourg

60-acre park featuring Medici Fountain, tree-lined promenades and sailboat basin.

💡 Insider Tip: Escalator access to the top floor offers a sweeping rooftop view over Paris including Notre-Dame and Montmartre.

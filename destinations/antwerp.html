Top 10 Highlights in This Route

Below are the handpicked venues included in this walking tour. Every location has been verified for quality and geographic proximity.

★ ★4.9
Landmark

1. Antwerpen-Centraal

Voted one of the most beautiful railway stations in the world, built in 1905 with monumental dome, sweeping marble staircases, and multi-level tracks.

💡 Insider Tip: 📸 Stand on the elevated mezzanine level to capture the grand clock, stained glass, and multi-tiered glass train shed in one shot.
★ ★4.8
Landmark

2. Onze-Lieve-Vrouwekathedraal

Largest Gothic cathedral in Low Countries featuring a 123m spire (UNESCO) and Peter Paul Rubens masterworks including Elevation of the Cross.

💡 Insider Tip: 🐕 In front of the cathedral entrance, look for the marble sculpture of Nello & Patrasche sleeping under a cobblestone blanket.
★ ★4.8
Landmark

3. Grote Markt & Brabo Fountain

Historic main square lined with 16th-century Renaissance guildhalls, City Hall, and bronze Brabo Fountain depicting the giant slayer legend.

💡 Insider Tip: 📸 The best photo composition frames the statue of Brabo in the foreground with the ornate guildhalls in the background.
★ ★4.8
Museum & Gallery

4. MAS | Museum aan de Stroom

60-meter red-sandstone and curved-glass tower in Eilandje docks exploring Antwerp's history as a world port with a free 10th-floor roof panorama.

💡 Insider Tip: 🌇 You do not need a museum ticket to ride the escalators up the glass boulevard to the 10th-floor rooftop panorama terrace.
★ ★4.6
Museum & Gallery

5. Rubenshuis

17th-century Italian-inspired palazzo and workshop where Peter Paul Rubens lived, painted, and hosted international dignitaries.

💡 Insider Tip: 🌿 Stroll through the newly restored Renaissance courtyard gardens and visitor center; advance online booking recommended.
★ ★4.8
Café & Bistro

6. The Chocolate Line

Housed inside former 18th-century Royal Palace on the Meir. Master chocolatier Dominique Persoone crafts experimental pralines.

💡 Insider Tip: 🍫 Walk through to the palace's historic royal kitchen in the back to watch chocolatiers temper chocolates in real time.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

7. Stadsbrouwerij De Koninck

Historic home of Antwerp's iconic amber ale 'Bolleke', featuring high-tech interactive brewery tour with suspension bridges over kettles.

💡 Insider Tip: 🍺 The self-guided tour ends in the lively bar with two fresh draft tastings served in the classic spherical bolleke glass.
★ ★4.6
Landmark

8. Het Steen

13th-century medieval fortress standing on Scheldt river—Antwerp's oldest building, housing The Antwerp Story attraction and roof deck.

💡 Insider Tip: 🚶 Climb the outdoor ramp to the free rooftop terrace; greet the quirky bronze statue of the giant Lange Wapper outside.
★ ★4.8
Museum & Gallery

9. Museum Plantin-Moretus

World's only museum with UNESCO World Heritage status. Intact 16th-century printing press house with original lead typefaces.

💡 Insider Tip: 📖 The historical library and peaceful central courtyard garden feel frozen in time; check for live Historic printing demonstrations.
★ ★4.8
Museum & Gallery

10. KMSKA

Monumental fine arts palace housing unmatched collection of Flemish masters from Van Eyck and Rubens to James Ensor in modern white galleries.

💡 Insider Tip: 🎨 Holds the largest James Ensor collection in the world; take time to explore the hidden Color Rooms inserted inside the historic shell.

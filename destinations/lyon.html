Top 10 Highlights in This Route

Below are the handpicked venues included in this walking tour. Every location has been verified for quality and geographic proximity.

★ ★4.8
Landmark

1. Basilique Notre-Dame de Fourvière

Iconic 19th-century hilltop basilica dominating Lyon's skyline with ornate mosaics and panoramic city views.

💡 Insider Tip: Take the funicular railway 'Funiculaire de Fourvière' up the hill. The panoramic view over Lyon's red roofs from the basilica terrace is unbeatable.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

2. Cathédrale Saint-Jean-Baptiste

Majestic Gothic cathedral in Vieux Lyon featuring a rare 14th-century astronomical clock.

💡 Insider Tip: Visit at 12:00, 14:00, 15:00, or 16:00 to see the 14th-century astronomical clock chime and activate its mechanical wooden figures.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

3. Ancient Theatre of Fourvière

Grand 15 BC Roman amphitheatre built into the Fourvière hillside, still hosting summer performing arts festivals.

💡 Insider Tip: Look for small brass plaques marked 'Traboule' next to heavy wooden doorways. The Longue Traboule (54 Rue Saint-Jean) passes through 4 courtyards!
★ ★4.6
Landmark

4. Place Bellecour

One of Europe's largest pedestrian plazas, centered around an equestrian statue of King Louis XIV.

💡 Insider Tip: The two grand Roman theatres are completely free to explore. Catch the Nuits de Fourvière outdoor festival if visiting in summer!
★ ★4.7
Landmark

5. Place des Terreaux & Bartholdi Fountain

Vibrant city plaza framed by the Hôtel de Ville and Fine Arts Museum, featuring Frédéric-Auguste Bartholdi's monumental fountain.

💡 Insider Tip: Stroll down Lyon's main pedestrian shopping street stretching between Place Bellecour and Place des Terreaux past Haussmann facades.
★ ★4.6
Café & Bistro

6. Les Halles de Lyon Paul Bocuse

The temple of Lyonnaise gastronomy featuring gourmet stalls selling Saint-Marcellin cheese, charcuterie, and fresh oysters.

💡 Insider Tip: The ultimate indoor food temple of Lyon! Sample Saint-Marcellin cheese from Mère Richard, oysters, and Praline tarts.
★ ★4.7
Café & Bistro

7. Historic Bouchon Lyonnais

Authentic certified Lyonnaise bistro serving traditional quenelles, tablier de sapeur, and local Beaujolais wine.

💡 Insider Tip: Authentic certified 'Bouchon Lyonnais'. Order the classic Tablier de sapeur (tripe), quenelle de brochet, and Beaujolais wine.
★ ★4.7
Landmark

8. Grand Hôtel-Dieu

Magnificent 12th-century former hospital restored into a luxury heritage landmark with courtyards, dining, and shops.

💡 Insider Tip: Stroll the inner cloisters of this 18th-century hospital converted into a luxury shopping arcade and grand dome cocktail bar.
★ ★4.5
Landmark

9. Amphitheatre of the Three Gauls

Historical Roman amphitheatre where delegates from Gallic tribes gathered in antiquity on Croix-Rousse hill.

💡 Insider Tip: Built in 19 BC where delegates of the 64 Gallic tribes met. Free to view from the street on the slopes of Croix-Rousse.
★ ★4.6
Landmark

10. Église Saint-Nizier

Striking Flamboyant Gothic church on the Presqu'île peninsula with asymmetric spires and crypt history.

💡 Insider Tip: Admire the two contrasting Gothic steeples (one stone, one brick) and the glowing white interior on Place Jacobins.
